Executive Summary

The opening: safe enterprise AI execution

Legion is entering the commercial market as regulated enterprises move from AI experimentation to deployment. The missing layer is not interest in AI. It is the guardrails, governance, auditability, and operating model required to let AI agents do real work safely.

Legion's advantage is that it enables AI agents to operate safely, with human oversight, auditability, and governed execution across enterprise systems. ALPS can help commercialize that advantage in markets with large unstructured-data problems, high compliance exposure, senior-level anxiety around AI governance, and immediate need for safe agentic workflows.

Core Market Problem

Enterprise AI is moving faster than governance

Situation

Enterprise leaders want AI agents that can search, analyze, draft, route, summarize, reconcile, monitor, and execute workflows across systems.

Complication

Most organizations do not have a safe operating model. AI is being introduced faster than governance can keep up, creating operational risk around access, actions, logging, validation, supervision, and shadow AI. This is the Open Claw problem: unmanaged or loosely governed AI tools create a powerful but uncontrolled execution environment.

Resolution

Legion should position itself as the platform that brings order to enterprise AI through governed, auditable, human-supervised agent execution across high-risk environments.

Commercial message

Legion gives regulated enterprises a safe way to move from AI experimentation to governed AI execution.

Competitive Market Definition

The battlefield is broader than agent platforms

Legion competes with agent platforms, workflow tools, enterprise search vendors, identity governance vendors, hyperscalers, mission AI platforms, and consulting-led implementation models. The winning platform allows enterprises to safely let AI agents do work, not merely answer questions.
