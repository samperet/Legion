Two approaches

Two paths to the same destination.
Different costs.

Path A

Internal promotion

  • 12 months to install
  • Continuity of culture and customer relationships
  • Risk: institutional myopia, no fresh perspective
  • Cost: roughly 0.8× revenue in transition friction
Path B · Recommended

External search

  • 18–24 months to install
  • Fresh perspective with industry credibility
  • Risk: cultural friction, identity dilution
  • Cost: roughly 1.4× revenue, paired with structural reset
